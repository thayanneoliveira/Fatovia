# Audiovisual director V1 (laboratory)
Plan from a structured script, homologated Santa R manifest and independent ASR report.
No paid API or publication. No new factual claims or invented prices/numbers. Rules map text to existing or planned visual asset classes; they are not general semantic intelligence. Unrecognized concepts require editorial review. Missing or suspect cue timing falls back to original block timing. A resulting plan is never a production authorization.
A future renderer consumes the typed scenes; it must validate the real assets, source coverage, caption safety and motion visually. Do not merge this isolated lab branch into main.
